📜 SAMURAI COMBAT GUIDE

⚔️ Basic Controls

  • WASD / Arrow Keys: Move Jin Sakai
  • Left Click / J: Fast Katana Slash
  • Right Click / K: Heavy Stance Strike (Breaks Guard)
  • Space / Q: Guard & Perfect Parry (Time it with the blue glint!)
  • Left Shift: Dodge / Evade Roll (Evades red unblockable attacks)
  • E: Heal using 1 Resolve orb

🥋 4 Combat Stances

  • [1] Stone (Iwa): Critical thrusts against Swordsmen & Ronin.
  • [2] Water (Mizu): Shield-breaker flurry against Shieldmen.
  • [3] Wind (Kaze): Sweeping arcs and kick against Spearmen.
  • [4] Moon (Tsuki): 360-degree spin against Mongol Brutes.

⚡ Mythic Skills & Standoff

  • [R] Heavenly Strike: High-speed unblockable lightning slash (1 Resolve).
  • [T] Dance of Wrath: Teleports and slices 3 enemies (3 Resolve).
  • [F] Kunai / [G] Smoke: Stuns or blinds crowds.
  • [V] Ghost Stance: 1-hit kill fury when meter fills.
  • Standoff: Hold Space/LMB, release right when the enemy charges!
